# Research reconciliation: three orthogonal responsibilities, one typed interchange

## A. What the attachments actually offer

**UCG** defines eight-field kernel `K=(A,Q,O,δ,⟦·⟧,Π,I,V)` inherited from Relational Generativity. Its core object is a `Grammar Unit Card` (GUC) with explicit alphabet kind, admitted/negative grammar, denotation, operator signatures, generator, witness adapters, proleptic records, machine-form ref and compiler-form ref. The five seed experiment groups are: E01 periodic table (Z-led), E02 small connected molecular bond graphs, E03 free trees/ring forms, E04 nomenclature, E05 common mathematics. Its named distinctive procedure is **Proleptics**: GENERATE → SEAL → WITNESS → RECOGNISE → ACCOUNT. UCG's 156-section mission is a future commission and is not itself proof of implementation.

**GRAMMATONOMIA** specifies variety packs `L_v=(VarietyID,Σ,O,T,P,M,X,E)+NegativeGrammar` and four depths Α sound, Β form, Γ phrase, Δ sense. It proposes seven *operator profiles*—Diaplectic, Catenary, Synthetic, Orbital, Positional, Componential, Holophrastic. These are research taxonomic proposals and can overlap; phonology, morphology, syntax, semantic composition require independent data per variety, not derivation from the bare alphabet. The document asserts Wave-1 running machines but supplies no standalone executable machine archive, source manifest or test log: **unverified in this handoff**.

**POLYIA** already describes computational atoms, typed ports, negative grammars, intermediate representation, a concierge and a composed machine *plan*. Its current v0.1 demonstration compiles a source `.poly` into a plan and view, not a full runtime or 100 language models. The broader architecture is proposed.

**Plicastratum** has rank-k symbolic words, certified folding/promote/unfold, harnesses and history-sensitive identity. **Division ∞** supplies multi-port typed higher-order hyperedges with evidence-bearing strands. **Division Knot** supplies canonical identities, negative-space representation and provenance independent of the six university doors. These are reusable semantics/adapters, not reasons to reinterpret all languages as strings or all physics as finite automata.

## C. Naming discipline

Use **Automaton-Oriented Generative Engineering (AOGE)** as the research umbrella (house construction), **Compositional Generative Automaton (CGA)** for one defined composite with executable transition semantics, **State-Carried Generation (SCG)** for `artifact + replay trace + evidence`, **Automaton Guidance Calculus (AGC)** for guarded admissibility/composition (only when equations are stated), and **Variational Generative Automaton (VGA)** for controlled, invariant-preserving parameterized output families. Avoid implying these are newly discovered established mathematical objects or first ever instances of automata-based software. UCG's **Proleptics** retains Claude's intended meaning. Muse's grammatical class names remain hers.

A generic generative *machine* may be an FST, PDA, graph-rewriter, term rewriting system, constraint solver, actor/process net, simulator or an explicitly bounded general program. Requiring all of these to be *finite-state* is a type error. State-machine **orchestration** of a general program does not make its underlying semantics regular.

## D. A rigorous shared example

`topology` is a word-level input to PHONEX/WAZNOMIA. A language pack may contribute attested IPA and morphological alternatives; UCG may contribute a naming/term grammar; Division Knot records `phonetic-near`, `translated-attested`, `etymological-hypothesis`, `contradicted-under`, `unknown` as different relation operators; POLYIA composes the validated paths.

A second case is UCG's IUPAC alkane nomenclature, where a name-to-molecular-graph compiler is independently testable. A third case is a three-domain essay (stem cells, adult media as a sociological/legal topic, corn agriculture): the content planner should generate **comparable**, **contrastive**, or **historically attested** bridges only, with explicit lack-of-evidence output. Language-specific grammar mechanisms may guide *internal* realization, but applying Turkish vowel harmony to English is not a reason to assert English grammaticality. A separate English surface grammar validates the final English artifact.

**Cross-domain promotion must be demonstrated, not just registered**: same component contract compiles and executes at least one linguistic case and one nonlinguistic science case; compare outputs and refusals with independent baselines and costs.

## E. Resolving the harness conflict

POLYIA-HARNESS/0.1 orchestrates source inventory, component extraction, formalization, assembly and releases. UCG-HARNESS/0.2 orchestrates grammar experiments and requires byte-recomputed receipts. Do not run two competing global authorities. POLYIA remains the supervisory lifecycle; UCG runs as a **nested submachine** under a typed child capability. Let `owner authority / filesystem grant / external writes` be defined only at the outer boundary. Child receipts must include parent commit/manifest hash and evidence hashes, and can't authorize external side effects.

UCG's proposed byte-level receipt verification is an improvement; specify its generalization as an explicit **new version** of POLYIA's harness, not an invisible rewrite. `state succeeded` is never equivalent to `receipt JSON exists`.
