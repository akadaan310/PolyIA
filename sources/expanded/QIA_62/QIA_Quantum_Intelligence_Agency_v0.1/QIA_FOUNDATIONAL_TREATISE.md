## 1. Founding question

Given a form *f* whose expression can be a word, sound, symbol, scientific name, equation, signal, scene, causal claim, image, compiled program, or measured physical state, can we:

- address its **representation** without accidentally assigning it a single meaning;
- factor it relative to a declared observer and preserve provenance;
- form a finite family of candidate lexical, semantic, conceptual, structural, mathematical and physical relations;
- type-check and compose those relations into executable paths with positive **and negative** witnesses;
- quotient or abstract a family into a reusable field and a potentially unbounded **grammar**;
- synthesize a valid physical scene/model/compiler target using deterministic inference when adequate operators are known;
- measure correctness, resources, coverage, loss, stability, and novelty against independent baselines?

These are distinct obligations. No substring, Latin root, binary prefix, shared thesaurus word, embeddings-based proximity, or quantum-state amplitude establishes scientific identity by itself.

## 2. Terminology—names, mapped names, maps and mappables

**Surface name** `n∈N_ℓ`: exact finite inscription in language/writing system ℓ. Includes whitespace, morphological derivations, syntax, alternative spellings and historical forms. A proper name may have no compositional semantic content.

**Denotation** `D_{c,t}⊆N×O`: a context/time-dependent *relation*, not universally a function. A name can denote several objects; an object can have many names. **Naming event** `ν=(actor,time,namespace,form,referent,authority,evidence)` registers one contextual assertion of denotation. *Named form* is the role of a surface in such an event. **Mapped name** is a name occurring as source or target of an explicitly typed nomenclatural relation, rather than a new linguistic species.

**Map** is reserved for a function `f:X→Y` with declared domain/codomain and unique output per input. **Correspondence** `R⊆X×Y` is used for ambiguous or evidence-conditioned links. **Mapping process** is a specified composable derivation `p` of relation instances with source contexts, admissibility predicate, evidence and resource accounting. **Mapping witness** is a machine-inspectable proof tree or empirical evidence package. **Mappable** is a form and port whose type satisfies at least one declared relation signature—*not* a promise that a semantic correspondence exists.

**Candidate name space** `S(n,c,B)` contains all forms *actually enumerated* under context `c`, generation grammar and budget `B`. The potentially unbounded set of strings licensed by a recursive grammar is mathematically definable but not an “infinite result” that has already been looked up or verified. **Nomenclatural fiber** of `R` at target `y` is `{x : x R y}` (possibly empty, nonunique, or infinite mathematically). **Nomenclatural quotient** groups surface forms only under an actual equivalence relation, not by `closeMatch` or a arbitrary similarity threshold without transitivity.

**Latinized representation** is a **notation and lexicon namespace**, never an ontological privileged layer. Distinguish (i) attested classical Latin lemma, (ii) grammatical Latin phrase composed for illustration, (iii) Medieval/Neo-Latin scientific terminology, (iv) Greek-derived learned medical combining form, (v) internationally standardized chemical nomenclature, and (vi) Latin-script transliteration of another language. These are not interchangeable.

**Quantum unit (Q-unit)**, house definition: `q=(form_id,sort,observable,chart,boundary,admissible_ops,certificate,granularity)`, an **observation-relative** irreducible interface atom for an explicit research task. Q-units can be lexical, logical, geometric, dynamical, acoustic, representational or relational. They are *not* subatomic particles, physical qubits, quantum fields in QFT, or universally smallest pieces of reality. Refinement `q→(q_i)` is allowed when it produces a new tested distinction at acceptable cost; otherwise stop, documenting the budget and observables. This extends IX's `atomic-under(c,observable,budget)` instead of inventing an absolute foundation beneath every substrate.

**Q-theory**, house definition: `T=(Σ, X, C, O, A, I, V, B, P)` where Σ is a typed signature, X its permissible states/objects, C contextual charts, O registered operators, A admission rules, I invariants, V verifiers/counterexample procedures, B resource and granularity budgets, P evidence. A “Q-theory” is a **falsifiable wrapper/contract** for each of 62 existing university topics, not automatic proof of a new theory. Full 62-record individual proposals appear in `schemas/Q_THEORIES_62.json` and `Q_THEORY_ATLAS_62.md`.

**QIA field** `\mathcal F_{c}` is a named, versioned, queryable view (a typed indexed family of Q-units and relations) parameterized by chart, observable, authority and admission policy. Contrast a **physical field** `φ:M→V` defined on spacetime or domain M; conflating the two is forbidden. A QIA field may *contain* a physical-field representation, but is not one by default.

**Q-compound** is a well-typed multi-input construction `ω(q_1,...,q_k) ↝ y` with ports, a derivation and conditions; “entanglement” here means a registered **correlation or relational incidence** unless a concrete Hilbert-space quantum model has been separately defined.

## 5. “Quantum” — three claims that must remain separate

**QIA quantum-like terminology (house)**: a system of Q-units, field views, amplitude-inspired scoring, nonlocal *representational* correlations and compositional paths. The first three words are names; they imply no physical quantum behavior.

**Finite-dimensional quantum mathematics (real, classically simulable)**: select an orthonormal basis `|q_i⟩` of a Hilbert space `ℂ^n`, state `|ψ⟩=Σ_i α_i|q_i⟩` with `Σ|α_i|²=1`, an explicitly defined Hermitian Hamiltonian H and unitary `U(t)=exp(-itH/ℏ)`, or a specified gate circuit. Measurement probabilities obey Born's rule. **The basis labels are chosen classical entities; the amplitude itself is not a semantic fact.** Unitary evolution does not generally model irreversible lexical inference or validated causality. A density operator and quantum channel can model noise/mixed states only when defined and checked (positive semidefinite, trace one; completely-positive trace-preserving maps).

**Physical quantum computation**: requires devices/qubits/circuit cost analysis and actual algorithm/oracle/readout model. Simulating Hilbert-space vectors on a CPU is **not** a quantum computational advantage. Quantum walks do not confer universal faster-than-graph-search traversal; even successful speedups have strong graph, oracle, structure and measurement assumptions. Tensor networks can compress restricted low-entanglement or low-rank structures, while worst-case bond dimensions can explode. A complex amplitude is not a stand-in for evidence strength.

**Quantum hypergraph** should mean either (a) an EFC hypergraph over classical Q-units, or (b) a separately specified quantum state/observable construction on a hypergraph (Hilbert space + operators + dynamics + measurement). Do not use the same type for both. A hypergraph with “strings” attached is not yet string theory in physics. Knot theory requires an embedding/isotopy problem; a cyclic relation in a graph is not by itself a knot.

**Concrete unitary toy (executable)**: for `|0⟩=(1,0)^T`, Hadamard `H=(1/√2)[[1,1],[1,-1]]` yields `H|0⟩=(|0⟩+|1⟩)/√2`, measured probabilities `(.5,.5)`; `H²|0⟩=|0⟩`, probabilities `(1,0)`. The intermediate alternatives interfere; collapsing into independent classical candidate probabilities and reapplying stochastic mixing generally cannot reproduce this same phase-coherent behavior. Our 2×2 CPU calculation demonstrates the algebra only. The 35+ prototype tests check normalization and cancellation, not computational performance gains.

### 6.1 The two houses

- Household namespace: `Head Pain Reliever` is a **functional expression/name** claiming an intended outcome. Parse `head(location) + pain(condition) + reliever(role/function)`; product identity is *not* entailed by those lexemes.
- Scientific/medical/pharmacy namespaces: `acetaminophen` is a chemical substance identity in the US naming scheme; international generic alias `paracetamol`. Its systematic name `N-(4-hydroxyphenyl)acetamide`, chemical formula `C8H9NO2` and full connectivity are external chemical claims carried by the original FNMS source. ### 6.3 Distinct forward, inverse and refusal paths

`Head Pain Reliever (surface)`

→ `[parse]` `head | pain | reliever`

→ `[sense-and-role composition: three inputs required]` `INTEND(reduce pain localized at head)`

→ `[target-effect]` `headache analgesia`

← `[supported clinical uses]` `{acetaminophen, ibuprofen, naproxen, ...}`

and **separately**:

`hypothetical product identity` → `[user stipulation]` `acetaminophen` → `[sourced alias]` `paracetamol` → `[sourced structure]` chemical connectivity.

The arrow into headache relief from a substance is **not invertible** to recover a unique substance from the effect. The three substances in the bundled toy are a small curated *known fiber*, not exhaustive. The tested new QIA runner returns `not-derivable` when asked to infer `acetaminophen` from the household phrase; with explicit product stipulation it returns a derivation certificate retaining that epistemic status.

**Negative witnesses**: (1) `head` and `acetyl` have no established etymological identity, (2) `pain reducing` does not identify a unique molecule, (3) sharing phenol/amide counts does not identify a ring substitution pattern, (4) Greek combining form is not classical Latin, (5) pharmacological effect is not proved by molecule substring.

**Antonym-like calculation**: define response metric `Δp=p(t_1)-p(t_0)` at specified patient, time, scale and conditions. Directional roles `relieves_pain` (Δp<0) versus `exacerbates_pain` (Δp>0) oppose on one ordered outcome dimension; `no_change` (Δp=0) is a third state. None of these is a claim that an existing analgesic causes the opposite response in patients. **True antonymy is typed and conditional, not Boolean complement on words.**

### 6.4 Entropy, projection and “continuous” semantic measurements

An exact string has bits, but **binary distance between UTF-8 strings is not meaning distance**. A lexeme parser can yield a finite distribution over senses *only after* an evidence-based probabilistic model has been specified. For `p(sense | name,c)` define entropy `H(S|name,c)=-Σp_i log₂ p_i` only when `p` exists and sums to one. Information gain is `H(S|context)-H(S|new evidence,context)` averaged over appropriate evidence distributions, not one arbitrary per-case score. Semantic embeddings may provide heuristic neighbors; they require held-out validation and are not proof paths.

A continuous optimization stage exists only after an actual metric/embedding and differentiable objective have been defined, e.g. learning operator parameters θ with regularized loss `L(θ)=L_retrieval+λL_type_violations+μL_calibration`. Discrete provenance, boolean type validity and derivative-based optimization are different mathematical layers. Projected names can be **unbounded by grammar** without pretending measured expansions are infinite.

### 8.2 Generative physics / generational physics / evolutionary physics

**Generative physics**: compile typed *law + parameter + boundary + geometry + solver + observable* into a runnable representative `M(θ)`. Example `Spring(m,k,c)` produces `m x¨+c x˙+kx=0` and an initial-value simulation. The emitted code must preserve dimensions and numerical guards.

**Generational physics**: explicitly *time-indexed family of models or world configurations* `M_{t+1}=Adapt(M_t,observations,constraints)` or successive procedural generations; define 'generation' in each application. It is **not** an established physics category on its own.

**Evolutionary generative physics**: search over typed models `θ_{g+1}=Select(Mutate(θ_g),fitness,physical_constraints)` under validation; a genetic algorithm or system identification can implement it. Empirical data, priors, conservation tests and held-out predictions are mandatory. Unlike nature's laws, a simulated world's authored rules may be changed deliberately; version every change and avoid declaring it a discovery of physical law.

**Simulation vs observation vs appearance:** A photorealistic frame need not derive from physically correct fluid/atmosphere dynamics, while a physically accurate simulation may have imperfect rendering. QIA's contracts need both. “100% physical-only” is an **ambition** incompatible with a finite real-time game that includes fictional/nonphysical interactions unless these are explicitly modeled as abstract computational entities alongside approximated physics.

## 9. String-completeness: a falsifiable alternative to an impossible absolute

In theoretical computer science, NP-completeness is a complexity-class reduction definition; **“string-complete” is not analogous merely because everything has links**. Physical string theory is also not a proof of complete interconnection of every game element. Propose instead **SC-B completeness relative to a finite declared basis**, not absolute infinity:

Let `U_B` be the finite inventory of modeled entities/processes/fields/events under budget/resolution `B`; `Required_B(x)` the declared ports and invariants for an object type; `W_B(x,p)` a validated witness that port `p` is bound to an admissible law, interaction, boundary, or explicitly marked external/environment port. Define

`SC_B(World) := ∀x∈U_B ∀p∈Required_B(x): ∃w∈W_B(x,p), valid(w)`.

The compiler may certify SC-B against the *declared schema* if it enumerates and checks every required port in that finite registry. It must report omissions and unknowns, not fabricate relationships. It does **not** entail every leaf interacts with every other leaf. Requiring complete pairwise connectivity would be physically wrong (causal locality and finite propagation) and computationally `Ω(n²)` under explicit enumeration. **Completeness of specified interaction contracts** is the rigorous target.

A representative memory/cost contract: entity resolution `ε_space, ε_time`, collision and acoustic frequency bands, observables tolerances, LOD replacement maps, latency and energy budgets. Choosing adaptive fidelity rather than uniformly simulating every atom is not a failure; it is the definitional requirement for a bounded machine. Continuous differential equations may be represented in finite symbolic code while numerical solutions remain discrete approximations.

## 10. The symbolic compiler pipeline without routine LLM inference

A local VM could host lexical evidence indexes and prevalidated domain theory rules. QIA's deterministic execution path is:

`Input Surface` → exact bytes/hash + source identity → language/script segmentation → lexicon candidate lookup → sense/context disambiguation with retained alternatives → typed n-ary hypergraph/path query → semiring or constraint search → counterexample/admissibility gate → factored Q-unit/field model → physics/math operator selection → dimension/type checker → solver choice/stability check → deterministic typed intermediate representation (IR) → compiler/backend (Python/C++/Unreal components) → execution telemetry → independent verification.

For `Input: a flexible leaf hit by wind while a camera sees it and a microphone receives a sound`, an IR might be:

```json
{
  "schema": "QIA/PhysicsIR/0.1-proposal",
  "entities": [{"id": "leaf-001", "kind": "thin_elastic_surface", "geometry_ref": "mesh:leaf", "material_ref": "constitutive:anisotropic_shell"}],
  "fields": [{"id": "wind", "sort": "velocity_field", "units": "m/s"}, {"id": "light", "sort": "radiance_field", "units": "W/(sr*m^2)"}],
  "operators": ["aerodynamic_force", "elastic_solve", "collision", "render_transport", "acoustic_propagation"],
  "ports": ["boundary_constraints", "time_step", "material_parameters", "observer_camera", "listener"],
  "constraints": ["typed_ports", "dimensional_consistency", "stability_budget", "evidence_tiers", "declared_approximation"],
  "status": "schema illustration: missing parameters prevent execution"

```

This is not a completed executable leaf simulation; any missing mesh, material, boundary, solver or sampling parameter must cause *typed refusal or an explicit default with provenance*. Known operator templates can compile deterministically without an LLM. An LLM can optionally propose an unfamiliar constitutive law or interface mapping—but a proposal is not admitted without type, physics and empirical verification. QIA thus moves the expensive **interpretation** cost to indexed preprocessing and admits a fast symbolic **execution** phase where models are already formalized; neither zero inference cost nor general intelligence is established.

### Example implemented in this packet

`SpringMassIR(m=1 kg, k=100 N/m, c=0.1 Ns/m, x=0.1 m, v=0 m/s, Δt=0.005 s)` is one typed, deterministic spring-mass scene primitive. A symplectic-Euler step computes acceleration and updates velocity then position. There is no adaptive timestep, manifold contact, collision, mesh, Unreal backend or automatic Lagrangian derivation yet. A minimal `DimQuantity` layer enforces `L,M,T` dimensional arithmetic, and an ideal free-field propagation toy computes sound delay `r/c` and inverse-square intensity. Tests validate those deliberately small capabilities only.

## 11. Field-map discovery and eventual nonrandom article selection

The 62 topic contracts give a finite first *coverage universe* `U`. Derive cross-topic motifs *before* selecting source articles:

- singular transformations (`same surface, distinct senses`), actual inverse fibers and negative examples;
- multi-input proof dependencies (3+ inputs), functional/semantically non-equivalent historical names;
- cross-field dimensional identities (wave, Fourier, acoustics, rendering, representation);
- true physical/causal constraints versus mere nomenclature resemblance;
- held-out counterexamples and source quality/license strata.

Candidate article `a` receives independently verified coverage vector `c(a,u)`, evidence level, provenance, licensing and cost. Select an explicitly diverse set by a **budgeted maximum coverage/submodular-like** objective **only if** the chosen objective actually has appropriate monotonic/submodular properties. Hyperedge witness bonuses, pairwise redundancy penalties and constraints may destroy those guarantees. Do not claim greedy approximation factors without proving their assumptions. Respect finite cardinality, e.g., a first goal of 10,000 articles only when attainable; a million articles is a scaling target, not a new category or automatic discovery of Division VII/VIII. Reserve both random and adversarial held-out samples; test against nearest-neighbor text similarity, keyword overlap, embeddings, blind ontology manual annotation, and ordinary typed graph-search baselines.

**A QIA-derived query** could request “all articles whose validated mathematics admits a map preserving units from a PDE sound-wave model to a game acoustic output, while registering aliasing and bandwidth constraints,” not “10,000 random Wikipedia pages.” The former yields an expected field-model family (wave physics/finite-difference/numerical stability/audio sampling/room propagation) before retrieval.

## 12. Performance hypothesis and falsification

Three separable hypotheses, none demonstrated by the current packet:

- **H1 Semantic quality:** type-checked operator chains produce fewer false medical/scientific identity bridges than string similarity. Metric: held-out false-positive rate at equal recall with provenance verified by experts.
- **H2 Compile speed:** for a finite repeated operator workload, precompiled field indices and IR reduce median and 99th percentile latency relative to repeated LLM completion with equal acceptance-test pass rate. Measure memory, indexing cost, cold-start time, GPU/CPU and incremental update cost.
- **H3 Quantum-inspired utility:** for specific low-rank or coherently structured tasks, finite tensor-network/quantum-walk-inspired computation may improve a stated accuracy/time/memory frontier over strong classical graph, Datalog, automata, database, and retrieval baselines. Require same hardware and measured complexity; a classical simulation of a quantum gate alone is not evidence.

Expected hard cases include polysemy, cross-lingual false friends, synonyms that stop matching after translation, rare physical constitutive laws, causal confounding, numerical instabilities, unbounded recursive expansions, imperfect article licensing, and adversarial paraphrases.

## 13. Topological and algebraic rigor

**Topology:** if a verified preorder of specialization `≤` exists on a set of lexical/concept forms, upward-closed subsets generate an Alexandrov topology; this is not the same as arbitrary graph connectedness. Other suitable topologies require construction and proofs. **Formal concept analysis:** from a source-validated incidence relation between Q-theories and features, use Galois closures for lattice candidates; presence of a shared word is not enough to share a physical law.

**Category/operad:** typed one-input arrows may form a category only after identity/associativity are established; multi-input operations warrant colored operads or multicategories with explicit composition laws. An EFC multiway hyperedge should not be flattened to pairwise claims that lose all-input admission. **Sheaves:** apply local/global gluing only where restriction maps and compatibility are genuinely defined. **Semiring provenance:** addition represents alternative proofs and multiplication joint derivation when a semiring is chosen; some provenance semirings are idempotent, others not, and scalar weights cannot silently become calibrated posterior probabilities. **Hilbert spaces:** functional analysis and quantum amplitudes apply when an inner product, normalization and relevant operators have been constructed; embedding arbitrary lexical strings in a vector store alone is not evidence of physics.

## 14. Validation ledger (as built)

Bundled prototype `src/qia.py` (stdlib only) and `tests/test_qia.py` produce **35 passing unit tests** on this execution environment. Verified **software** properties: 62 distinct source topic IDs with six-division distribution, exact UTF-8 round trips and NFC divergence, dictionary-backed lexical candidates with Latin/Greek separation, n-ary required-port admission, missing-evidence/status/type refusal, known inverse-fiber nonuniqueness, no name-to-molecule entailment, explicitly stipulated product identity, trace-carrying minimum-cost tree derivations, 2×2 unitary norm/interference, a finite spring timestep plus numerical energy-error bound on one case, ideal acoustic delay/intensity, basic dimensional-consistency rejection, and finite formal-context closure idempotence.

Original archive test counts **are not** included as successes of the new 35-test suite; they are separate prior records. Runtime tests are demonstration checks, not a formal verification theorem. ## 16. User-facing research conclusion

**Novel house conjecture:** a single typed, evidence-conditioned operator atlas might serve both **semantic nomenclature discovery** and **constructive physics compilation** by sharing forms, sorts, context charts, incidence hyperedges, refutation witnesses, and a low-cost symbolic execution kernel. The attempt is more specific and testable than labeling arbitrary concepts “quantum particles”; nevertheless its utility and research novelty still require rigorous comparative experiments.

The key correction to the founding ambition: a substrate-relative Q-unit is not an objectively “smaller-than-everything” physical particle. It is the smallest meaningful/verified **interface for a chosen observable and budget**, from which larger constructs can be composed and compiled. This supports substructural reasoning without the impossible requirement of atomically simulating all reality in a realtime game.
