# SNAD v0.1 research instrument

The supplied SNAD mission brief is the binding specification and authorizes end-to-end implementation in an independent repository. The instrument serves investigators comparing attribution paths and testing what surface grammar can and cannot establish. The original ISNAAD and FALAK systems remain untouched.

## Architecture and alternatives

Use a dependency-free, browser-compatible TypeScript SDK, a static Vite laboratory, and offline-first JSON artifacts. This permits clean checkout replay and operation without accounts. A server/database design would add identity and deployment dependencies without helping the current computational experiments. A fork of ISNAAD would inherit corpus exclusions and blur scientific independence; retain only pinned compatibility evidence and independently implement the domain-neutral model.

The SDK separates source records, immutable input units, grammatical features, attribution states, quotation frames, transitions, provenance evidence, uncertainty, and derived numerical summaries. Input identities depend on source loci and unit identifiers, not inferred attributes. Unknown speaker/addressee/referents remain explicit. Timestamps and uncertainty are preserved. Core methods are pure; streaming yields independent analysis objects. Language adapters preserve text exactly.

## Corpus and evidence

All 6,236 ayahs across 114 surahs have a canonical quran:S:A locus and roundtrip lookup. Empty features and one-word verses are valid. Source orthography is never normalized in storage. Morphology, when available, remains separately attributed. Addressable, text-parsed, analyzed, detected, visualized, and audio-verified denominators are separate. Audio verification starts at zero. Original ISNAAD selection is a pinned comparison, never the universe.

Observed, derived, hypothesis, and interpretation are distinct evidence types. Grammatical-person changes do not establish speaker changes. Quote cues and lexical rules carry method and uncertainty. Invariants, counterexamples, deterministic perturbations, structural signatures and simple baselines are computational claims only. No inferred speaker intent or theological interpretation is verified.

## Laboratory

A restrained editorial research desk: warm paper, dark ink, teal evidence accents, clear typography. Guided examples include the required loci, free locus lookup over the entire corpus, source spans, a directed attribution path, transition details, provenance/uncertainty, a perturbation comparison, general dialogue editing and portable export. Responsive layout, keyboard access, labelled controls and accessible text equivalents for graphs are required. No astronomical imagery.

## Experiments and continuation

Whole-corpus runtime/memory and counts are measured. Finite generated tests examine identity, mutation isolation, deterministic replay, roundtrip and composition laws. Research records distinguish tested propositions from open hypotheses and candidate observations. Negative results are preserved. PURL/CGIT are optional integration transports discovered from their actual contracts; the previous mission is never mutated. A new scoped mission is created only through supported credentials. Otherwise a content-hashed offline manifest, checkpoint, objectives, source references and publication procedure form the continuation artifact.

## Verification and limits

Run SDK/property tests, complete identity enumeration, corpus passes, production build, browser journeys and automated accessibility checks, then clean-checkout replay. A fresh-context agent receives only the mission pointer and must locate code/evidence and its next permitted operation. Automated agent review is not an independent human usability session. Stretch research targets must not be filled with invented discoveries. Commit/push independent SNAD; publish a preview if supported within the user's authorization. Missing licensing, auth, service functionality and human validation remain explicit blockers.
