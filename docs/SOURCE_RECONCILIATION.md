# Source custody and constitutional reconciliation

## The adopted rule

The current owner directive governs this commission. **Infinity is IX; VII and VIII are open research charters.** Option A includes the formal vocabulary, division charters and scientific obligations from B, and the produce/verify state system, acknowledged guidance and complete working commission interface from C. The machine service is Codex's first operational deliverable. The core product runtime must work without LLM inference. These decisions are transcribed in `sources/current/OWNER_DIRECTIVE_2026-10-24.md` and recorded in `registry/authority.json`. The initial dictated intuition, including its repetition, is separately preserved as `sources/current/OWNER_INTUITION_TRANSCRIPT.md`; `docs/OWNER_INTENT_CROSSWALK.md` maps 31 substantive intentions to the commission. Both are chat-text transcriptions with stated custody scope, not claims of original message-transport byte identity.

Source custody and scientific acceptance are independent. The owner states that the University corpus has already been hand-reviewed. This package preserves that authority and every available source byte. It does not claim that every inherited program has passed the new verifier or that every source proposition has been formally proved. A source can be canonical, historically reviewed and still require a new execution receipt, a scope correction or an independent witness.

## How inherited obligations are retained

`registry/inherited_obligations.json` has an individual record for all **96 original PolyIA clauses and 156 UCG sections**. Each record retains the original text, original source hash, exact source path/lines, stable origin ID, disposition, current PIA targets, reason and acceptance status. `docs/INHERITED_CROSSWALK.md` is a human reading projection. The retained original acceptance details remain binding unless an identified amendment refines them; a broad new clause does not delete the original quantitative or method-specific requirement.

`registry/inherited_supplemental.json` covers additional constitutions, seeds, master directives, admission contracts and relevant repository rules. It preserves exact section text and aliases for duplicate files. This includes the Astra, TRE/TREE, PILLAR9, CIA Browser, PHONEX, Q-Strings, public University and MUSA source materials and the non-numbered material around the two primary constitutions. Specialized upstream product requirements are scoped to their actual project/adapters, rather than being erased or falsely represented as completed by Poly IA.

Dispositions mean:

| Disposition | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `retained` | The original obligation and its acceptance detail remain active under identified PIA targets. |
| `refined` | A specific ambiguity, false implication, changed implementation context or current authority is clarified with a linked rationale. The original remains preserved. |
| `superseded_by_owner` | The current owner's explicit decision replaces the identified conflicting part. Other parts remain active. |
| `scoped_to_upstream` | The obligation remains a contract of its original project; Poly IA inherits relevant adapter/authority/custody duties, not a false claim to have built that entire external product. |
| `unresolved` | Required identity, interpretation or source evidence remains absent and must be resolved before the dependent acceptance claim. |
