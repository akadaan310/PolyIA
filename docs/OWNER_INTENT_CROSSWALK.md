## Interpretation and limits

The source's scientific analogies remain visible in their original wording. Their operational meaning is defined with types, domains, evidence and costs. For example, asymptotic constant cost is normally written O(1); O(2) denotes the same asymptotic class rather than logarithmic growth. A chemical formula can be parsed and reasoned about under a declared chemical grammar, while its formula alone is not a preparation program or a complete molecular structure. Q-string terminology in this commission does not assert physical quantum computation. These clarifications preserve the intended research direction while making acceptance falsifiable. See `docs/SCIENTIFIC_FOUNDATIONS.md` for the full treatment and source references.

A crosswalk proves that an intention has a destination and a verification obligation. It does not prove that the future commission has already satisfied that obligation. The package supplies a working bootstrap and exact bounded scientific seeds; full product completion remains governed by the actual acceptance system and source-linked evidence.

The separately named **Book of Forms** still has an unresolved exact identity. Its related preserved publications and the complete pinned repository corpus are inventoried without silently substituting one title for another. The transcription also retains historical informal names; resolved identities and unresolved alternatives belong in the reconciliation register.
